About BHOOMI

Smart Soil and Crop Monitoring System

BHOOMI is a smart soil and crop monitoring system built as a final year ECE project. It uses real sensors placed in the soil to read conditions like pH, moisture, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, electrical conductivity and soil temperature.

Based on these readings, combined with the soil type reported by the farmer, BHOOMI compares the actual soil profile against a database of crop requirements and tells the farmer which crops are suitable, and why some crops are not suitable for their current soil condition.

Our Features

  • Soil Analyze - run a quick 3 minute soil test and get crop suitability results
  • Plant Monitoring - continuously watch a selected crop and get instant alerts if soil conditions go outside the safe range
